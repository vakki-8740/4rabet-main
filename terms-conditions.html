Terms & Conditions

These terms cover the use of this support website (4R BET Support Center). When you submit a complaint here or use the online chat, you agree to the points below. Everything is written in plain language and point by point — please read it once.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1 How these terms apply

  • This support page exists to help 4R BET users — deposits, withdrawals, email verification, identity verification and live chat.
  • You do not need an account to submit a complaint. Your details are used only for the record of that complaint.
  • The final decision on every request is taken by the team in line with legal requirements and safety rules.

2 The information you provide

  • 1 Details must be accurate and complete. The user name, mobile number, email, amount and problem details must match what is on your account. Incorrect details can get a complaint rejected or delayed.
  • 2 Enter login details only in this form. The password is used only for verification. Never send it to anyone on WhatsApp, Telegram or through an unknown link.
  • 3 Documents and screenshots must be your own. Do not upload someone else's ID, screenshot or bank details. Doing so will get the complaint closed.
  • 4 One complaint per problem. Sending the same complaint again and again slows your case down instead of speeding it up. Check your Mailbox for the status.

3 Complaint process and timings

  • 1 The record is created on submit. Your complaint is saved with a complaint ID and appears in your Mailbox — even on a slow connection.
  • 2 Status: Pending → Previewing → Successful. Pending means it is in the queue, Previewing means the team is working on it, Successful means the work is complete.
  • 3 Normal response time: 15 to 30 minutes. Cases that need review (such as ID verification or a large withdrawal) can take a little longer.
  • 4 There is no extra charge. Support is free. Any message asking you for money is fraud — ignore it and report it here.

4 What is not allowed

  • Filing a fake complaint or giving false details — for example inflating the amount deliberately or using someone else's account details.
  • Uploading another person's photo, ID or document without permission.
  • Using abusive language, threats or behaving rudely with an agent in chat.
  • Sending repeated spam complaints or filling the form with bots or tools.
  • Attempting to hack, copy or damage this website.
Please note: if anything listed above is done, your complaint may be closed without notice and future support may be restricted.

5 How your data is stored

  • 1 Complaints and chat records are stored in the cloud. This lets the team see your complaint from any device and keeps the record from being lost.
  • 2 A copy is also kept on your device. That is why your complaint is still saved and visible in your Mailbox when you are offline.
  • 3 Only the support team sees your complaint. Your record is never sold or shared with a third party. Sharing your details with anyone else remains your own responsibility.
  • 4 Clearing your browsing data removes the device copy. So note your complaint ID somewhere safe — that is what the team uses to find your complaint.

6 Outcome and responsibility

  • The team makes every effort on its side, but not every complaint can end the same way — some cases may be rejected as per the rules.
  • Any loss caused by incorrect details, an incomplete screenshot or a fake document is the user's own responsibility.
  • The complaint status is the official answer — do not treat a personal message from any individual as official.

7 Changes to these terms

  • These terms may be updated when needed. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when they last changed.
  • Continuing to use the website means you agree to the updated terms.
  • If you have any question, ask us on Online Chat — the team will explain it in plain language.
