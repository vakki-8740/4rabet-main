About Us

4R BET Support Center is a helpdesk where you can report any problem related to deposits, withdrawals, email verification and identity verification. You do not just get a form here — you get a full record of your complaint, its live status and real chat support, so you never have to explain the same thing twice.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1 Who we are

  • 1 The official support helpdesk for 4R BET. We have one job: get your problem solved as quickly as possible, without sending you from one person to another.
  • 2 All your support in one place. Deposits, withdrawals, email verification, identity verification and live chat — everything on this one website. No extra app or link to hunt for.
  • 3 Every complaint is kept on record. Once you submit a complaint it appears in your Mailbox with its complaint ID, date, time and current status.
  • 4 Our team keeps you updated. Whenever work moves forward on your complaint, the status changes — from Pending to Previewing, and then to Successful.

2 What we can help you with

Deposit Problem

Money was deducted but never reached your account, a deposit is stuck as pending, or you want to report it with a payment screenshot — start here.

Withdrawal Problem

A withdrawal request is pending, the amount never arrived, or your bank/UPI details look wrong — file a complaint right away.

Email Verification

The verification link never reached your inbox, the code expired, or the email was entered incorrectly — we get it verified again.

Identity Verification

Your ID proof will not submit, the document was rejected, or the photo is not clear — we guide you and get it submitted again.

Online Chat

When a form is not enough or you need an answer fast — talk to an agent directly, with text, photos or files.

Status for every complaint

Your Mailbox always shows the count of Total, Pending, Previewing and Successful complaints — nothing is hidden.

3 How a complaint works (4 easy steps)

  • 1 Pick your problem. On the home page open the card that matches your issue — deposit, withdrawal, email or identity.
  • 2 Fill in the form. Enter your user name, mobile number, email, amount and the details of the problem. Where a screenshot is asked for, upload a clear image.
  • 3 You get a complaint ID. The moment you submit, your complaint is saved and a complaint ID is created. That ID appears in your Mailbox straight away.
  • 4 Keep an eye on the status. Open your Mailbox — as the work finishes the status moves to Successful. Need help in between? Use Online Chat.

4 What each status means

  • Pending — your complaint has reached our team and is in the queue. Most complaints are settled on their own at this stage.
  • Previewing — the team is actively working on your complaint. Screenshots are being checked or details are being verified. There is no need to send a reminder at this point.
  • Successful — the work is done. The deposit is credited, the withdrawal is released, or the verification is approved — the status in your Mailbox updates accordingly.
Most cases are settled within 15 to 30 minutes. If your complaint needs a deeper review, the status will stay on Previewing for a while — please be patient and do not open a new complaint for the same issue.

5 Your safety is our responsibility

  • 1 Enter your details only on this website. Never send login details or screenshots to a WhatsApp group, a Telegram channel or any unknown link. Official support exists only here.
  • 2 Nobody will ever ask you for money. Support is free of charge. If someone says "pay to get it done faster", that is fraud — report it immediately.
  • 3 Keep your complaint ID safe. Sharing the complaint ID in chat moves your case forward much faster.
  • 4 Correct details give the right result. A wrong mobile number or a wrong amount can get the complaint rejected and simply wastes time.

6 Support hours and reach

  • Online Chat: open 24 x 7 — talk to us whenever you need.
  • Complaint forms: available at any time of day, the record is created instantly.
  • Response time: usually 15–30 minutes, a little longer during peak hours.
  • Languages: English and Hindi — write in whichever you are comfortable with.
